The Architecture of Unbreakable Trust: Dual-Collateral Mechanics

By forcing both parties to have skin in the game, Escrow Sonet mathematically eliminates fraud before it can even start. No banks, no borders, no counterparty risk.

STAGE 1: THE BUYER DEPOSIT

Escrow Deposit Address A

The buyer deposits the full Trade Amount + a designated safety collateral percentage (e.g., 10%) into a unique tracking address. This proves financial commitment and secures the funds upfront, visible immediately to the seller.

STAGE 2: THE SELLER DEPOSIT

Escrow Deposit Address B

The seller deposits a matching safety collateral percentage (10%) into a completely separate address. Because neither party ever sees the other's personal wallet address, complete financial privacy is preserved.

Game Theory: Why It Beats Legacy Escrows

Zero Ghost Sellers

A seller cannot take the money and vanish. Doing so instantly forfeits their locked collateral, turning a fraudulent attempt into a guaranteed financial loss.

Zero Chargeback Fraud

Buyers cannot falsely claim they didn't receive an item to force a malicious refund. The tied collateral locks both parties until independent verification clears.

Perfect Alignment

When both sides hold locked capital inside the secure Escrow Sonet ecosystem, mathematical game theory forces honest communication and flawless delivery.
